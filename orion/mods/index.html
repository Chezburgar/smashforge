Using a pack

  1. Download it

    Press Download on any pack. You get the .zip exactly as it was published.

  2. Load it in the game

    Launch the client, then Options → Resource Packs and add the zip. It stays in that browser afterwards.

Match the version: a pack listed for 1.8 will not load on 1.12.2, and the other way round. That is what the badges on each pack are telling you.
